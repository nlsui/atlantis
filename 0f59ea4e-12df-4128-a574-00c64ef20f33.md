[[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]] is not governed democratically. Power lies with corporations — a small number of powerful entities that effectively control the city's infrastructure, resources, and decision-making. The result is something closer to a corporate oligarchy than a civic government.
