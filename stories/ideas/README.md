# Ideas

Raw and developing material — seeds, plotlines, character concepts, scenes, and drafts. Nothing here needs to be structured yet. When something is ready to become a full multi-chapter story, promote it into `../arcs/`.

- `seeds/` — raw inspirations, half-formed "what if"s.
- `plotlines/` — developed arcs with some shape (setup, turn, consequence), not yet broken into chapters.
- `characters/` — character concepts for stories, not yet canon.
- `scenes/` — vivid moments or images worth keeping, even without a plot around them.
