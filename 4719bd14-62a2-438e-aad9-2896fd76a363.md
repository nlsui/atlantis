The Festival is a beloved, highly anticipated annual gathering held for two days at [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]] in [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]].

## Life without assistance
For those two days, people inside the dome experience life without AI or particle-dust assistance: nothing is sensed, predicted, or quietly handled in the background. Everything is unmediated.

## Cultural meaning
The contrast with daily life's frictionless ease is valued as a rare, prized occasion rather than experienced as hardship. Two days without ambient assistance make the gathering the city's biggest yearly tradition. Its relationship to the population's confidence and dependence on AI is described in [[056ae6dd-1c03-4b3a-9e2a-f59082dab862|Character & Attitude]].
