The Festival is a beloved, highly anticipated annual gathering held for two days at [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]] in [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]].

## Life without assistance

For those two days, people inside the dome experience life without the usual technological assistance. Everything is unmediated.

## Cultural meaning

The contrast with daily life's frictionless ease is valued as a rare, prized occasion rather than experienced as hardship. Two days without ambient assistance make the gathering the city's biggest yearly tradition.
