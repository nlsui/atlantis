Beyond the walls of [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]] lies open, largely unspoiled nature: rolling grasslands and forest, nearby mountains, and the sea within reach.

## Coast
Atlantis is near the coast. The sea is a defining feature of the city's identity.

## Mountains
One or more mountains rise in the vicinity, providing elevation and geographical contrast.

## Grasslands and forest
Open grass and woodland form a lush, green landscape outside the urban environment.

## Wildlife and danger
[[0a33431b-a93e-439c-9904-997bbe09e3fe|Monsters]] inhabit the wilderness. Their presence is the primary reason for the city's [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]].
