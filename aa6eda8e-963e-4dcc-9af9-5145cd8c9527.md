The Dome is a large enclosed park somewhere within [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]]. It is deliberately kept free of [[6adf0130-83d0-4b37-a8a2-62f745dc64c4|Ubiquitous Particle Dust]]: there is no sensing, actuation, or AI-mediated assistance. It is the only place in Atlantis without particle dust.

## The park
- A genuine green park — trees, open grass, space to breathe — not an ornamental garden
- Large enough to comfortably host enormous crowds
- Enclosed by the dome, which is what excludes the particle dust from the space

## The annual gathering
The park hosts [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]], a two-day annual gathering centered on experiencing life without AI or particle-dust assistance.
