The Dome is a large enclosed park somewhere within [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]], preserved as a place without the usual technological assistance.

## The park

- A genuine green park with trees, open grass, and space to breathe
- Large enough to comfortably host enormous crowds
- Enclosed by a dome

## The annual gathering

The park hosts [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]], a two-day annual gathering centered on experiencing life without the usual technological assistance.
