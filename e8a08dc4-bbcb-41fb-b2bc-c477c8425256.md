Following the [[7bf708eb-a5e9-404a-9ba5-a475fcae28dc|End of the Gene Wars]], human genetic modification and research directed toward modifying the human genome were banned.

## Scope of the prohibition

The prohibition covers all deliberate modification of human genes, including treatments for illness as well as changes intended to alter capabilities or other traits. Medical benefit does not make an intervention permissible.

The prohibition remains an important rule in present-day Atlantis. Its existence and comprehensive scope are common knowledge among Atlantis inhabitants.
