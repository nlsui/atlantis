# Worldbuilding Meta Notes

These are inspirations and anchors for the world — not in-world lore, just context for us as builders.

## The Private City Concept
The founding and nature of Atlantis is loosely inspired by real-world ideas around privately built cities — billionaire-funded, designed from scratch to be ideal environments. This informs the city's wealth, its corporate power structure, and its sense of being a curated, self-contained world. This is **not** in-world knowledge — no character would describe their city this way.
