In [[e3429e96-f055-4be9-9cb5-e400d1b7e628|Beyond the City]], nature is not just beautiful — it is dangerous. Monsters inhabit the outside world, making the wilderness a genuine threat to the city and anyone who ventures into it.

The existence of monsters is the primary reason Atlantis maintains an organized [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]]. They are the conflict at the edge of the utopia.
