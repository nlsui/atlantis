## Location
Atlantis exists on Earth, but its exact geographic location is unknown. It sits somewhere with a warm but temperate climate: pleasant and mild, without the heat or humidity of the tropics.

## The Only City
To everyone who lives in Atlantis, it is the only city in the world. There are no other known cities, no distant metropolises to compare it to, no foreign urban civilization. Whether other cities exist somewhere beyond the known world is an open question — but within the worldview of Atlantis's inhabitants, their city stands alone.

## Climate
- Warm but not tropical
- Temperate and livable year-round
- The surrounding nature — grasslands, forests, mountains, sea — reflects this: lush and green, not arid or frozen

The surrounding landscape is described in [[e3429e96-f055-4be9-9cb5-e400d1b7e628|Beyond the City]].

## Quarters
The city is divided into quarters, each shaped by the creative vision of an Architect who controls its aesthetic and atmosphere. See [[d28e126f-5ce7-412d-ade3-26c5b955f079|Quarters]] for details.

## Landmarks
Not everywhere in the city is a quarter. [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]] is a large enclosed park and home to [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]], an annual gathering built around that fact.
