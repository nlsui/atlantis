## Genetic modification and unrest

Scientific advances enabled humanity to deliberately modify human genes. Such interventions could alter traits including physical strength and lifespan. The resulting differences generated social unrest that escalated into a major war between genetically modified and unmodified humans.

The conflict culminated in the [[7bf708eb-a5e9-404a-9ba5-a475fcae28dc|End of the Gene Wars]]. Its broad history is common knowledge among inhabitants of Atlantis.
