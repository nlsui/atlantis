Questions and working ideas for geography and the creatures beyond Atlantis. These are author tasks, not established world facts.

## [[e3429e96-f055-4be9-9cb5-e400d1b7e628|Beyond the City]]

### Mountains
- How many mountains are there?
- Do they have names?
- Where are they positioned relative to the city — backdrop to one side, surrounding on multiple sides?
- Are they accessible / do people venture into them?

### Coastline
- How close is the sea to the city walls?
- Is there a harbor, beach, cliffs?
- Does the coastline run along one side of the city or wrap around it?

### Grasslands & Forest
- What is the rough ratio of open grass to forest?
- Are they separate zones (e.g. forest to the north, plains to the south) or intermixed?
- Are there any notable landmarks within the nature — rivers, lakes, clearings?

### General
- Is the nature outside the city protected or wild? Are there people living out there?
- How far does the "known" world extend before it becomes uncharted territory?


## [[d28e126f-5ce7-412d-ade3-26c5b955f079|Quarters]]

- What formally counts as "abandoned enough" for a quarter to be up for claim — a hard threshold, or a judgment call by the Guild?
- When multiple aspirants want the same vacant or newly opened quarter, how is it decided — a formal contest, a Guild vote, first-mover advantage?
- Is founding/claiming a quarter the primary way a sub-architect becomes a lead Architect, or are there other paths?
- Does the Guild have powers beyond assignment — discipline, expulsion, quality control over sub-architects?
- Are there legendary quarters that have changed hands multiple times, carrying their own inherited prestige?
- How many quarters exist today, and what eras or aesthetics do they cover?

### Working example
The original quarter description suggested a district styled after 1800s London, using particle dust for permanent low fog, gaslamp-like light, damp air, and ambient sound. This is a working design example, not an established named quarter or an assigned character.

## [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]]

- What are the park and the festival actually called?
- Who founded or preserves the dome, and is that history documented anywhere?
- What do people actually do during the festival — structured rituals and games, or something more free-form?
- Is attendance universal, by invitation, by lottery, or simply open to whoever shows up?
- Does anything need to be arranged practically to run the city (or just that zone) without AI assistance for two days?
- Does the disconnection extend to personal devices people bring in, or only to the ambient city infrastructure?
- Is the festival universally embraced, or is there any tension/resistance to it?

The Dome and Festival are working names. The Dome's precise location within the city was deliberately left unspecified. Its preservation may be tied to the city's founding; that connection is a suggestion, not established history. Questions about the Festival's name, activities, attendance, devices, practical arrangements, and reception concern [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]]; questions about the park's name and preservation concern [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]].

## [[0a33431b-a93e-439c-9904-997bbe09e3fe|Monsters]]

- What do the monsters look like? Their hidden human origin is established, but their present forms remain undefined.
- Are they numerous, rare, or somewhere in between?
- Do they actively approach the city, or must defenders go out to meet them?
- Do they have intelligence, or are they purely instinct-driven?
- [x] Establish their hidden origin: mutated humans originating from genetically modified survivors of the Gene Wars. This is not inhabitants' knowledge.
- [x] Establish that the monsters' existence directly results from the Gene Wars.
- The timing of emergence may be developed if needed. Original-survivor versus later-generation status is a low-priority, case-by-case detail; see the Gene Wars — To-Do note.

### Other tentative details
- The sea may influence culture, trade, and history; those roles have not been established.
- Specific mountains, coastline features, named quarters, and their Architects remain undefined.

The precise location of Atlantis on Earth is deliberately left unspecified by the author.
