## Reference present

The provisional reference present is approximately year 1449 in an in-world calendar. This is not the contemporary real-world calendar. This choice replaces the earlier suggestions of 6000 and 1678; those suggestions were not established dates.

Years are counted from the end of the Gene Wars, also called the Genetic Wars. [[7bf708eb-a5e9-404a-9ba5-a475fcae28dc|End of the Gene Wars]] marks year zero. The wars' causes, participants, course, and other details are intentionally left undefined. The calendar's name, year length, months, day numbering, and the date within the reference year remain unspecified. The reference year and epoch can be revised later as consistent authoring corrections.

## Events before year zero

Events before year zero remain undated and are ordered through the framework's `after` links. Use branching where order between independent events is unknown; do not invent dates or elapsed durations. The epoch event anchors this ordering; no specific pre-zero events have been established yet.

## Multiple calendars

Multiple in-world calendars are anticipated. Their definitions and conversions remain open. Do not mix dates from different calendars in the framework's single `date` field: it does not interpret calendar identities or perform conversions.

## Framework use

This note records an authoring convention; it does not automatically set a framework-wide present or date-filter the wiki. The epoch event records `calendarYear: 0` as descriptive metadata. Its native `date` is omitted because day numbering has not been defined; `calendarYear` does not drive the framework's date sorting or filtering. Other lore remains undated. An event's native `date` requires both year and day; do not invent a day to encode an approximate year. Use relative ordering until sufficient dating information and a consistent reference calendar are established.
