## Reference present

The provisional reference present is approximately year 1449 in an in-world calendar. This is not the contemporary real-world calendar. The year number is measured from the end of the Gene Wars, not from the real-world calendar.

Years are counted from the end of the Gene Wars, also called the Genetic Wars. [[7bf708eb-a5e9-404a-9ba5-a475fcae28dc|End of the Gene Wars]] marks year zero. The public history now establishes genetic modification, ensuing unrest, a war between modified and unmodified humans, victory for the unmodified side, and a subsequent prohibition on modifying human genes. Specific factions, campaigns, the exact ending event, and the corresponding real-world dates remain undefined. The calendar's name, year length, months, day numbering, and the date within the reference year remain unspecified. The reference year and epoch can be revised later as consistent authoring corrections.

## Relationship to the real-world calendar

The author's reference point is real-world year 2026. The Gene Wars take place in the future relative to that point, at a date that has not been chosen. From Atlantis's present, approximately year 1449, the wars ended 1,449 years earlier. Atlantis year 1449 is therefore not real-world year 1449; the real-world year of Atlantis's present is not established. These are authoring coordinates, not an additional calendar inhabitants are assumed to use.

## Events before year zero

Events before year zero remain undated and are ordered through the framework's `after` links. Use branching where order between independent events is unknown; do not invent dates or elapsed durations. The epoch event anchors this ordering; the Gene Wars precede their end at year zero. Do not assign them a start year or duration until established.

## Multiple calendars

Multiple in-world calendars are anticipated. Their definitions and conversions remain open. Do not mix dates from different calendars in the framework's single `date` field: it does not interpret calendar identities or perform conversions.

## Framework use

This note records an authoring convention; it does not automatically set a framework-wide present or date-filter the wiki. The epoch event records `calendarYear: 0` as descriptive metadata. Its native `date` is omitted because day numbering has not been defined; `calendarYear` does not drive the framework's date sorting or filtering. The Gene Wars and the later prohibition event remain undated, ordered through `after` links. An event's native `date` requires both year and day; do not invent a day to encode an approximate year. Use relative ordering until sufficient dating information and a consistent reference calendar are established.
