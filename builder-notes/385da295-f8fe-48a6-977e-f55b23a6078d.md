Research tasks and unanswered technical questions. Keep these separate from established fictional capabilities.

## [[ad7aaa44-b455-414e-a43e-9de1b8ca6d65|Technology Overview]]

- What is the general aesthetic of the technology — sleek and minimal, organic, industrial?
- Are there visible signs of advanced tech in everyday life (transport, buildings, communication)?
- Who develops and maintains the technology — corporations, the AI systems themselves?
- Is there any technology that is restricted or inaccessible to ordinary citizens?

## [[bf823195-c261-4d27-9649-b2c4970787c8|AI Infrastructure]]

- Who built these systems — are they a legacy of the city's founders?
- Who has oversight or access to them now — the corporations?
- Are any of the AI systems known to the public by name or reputation?
- Are there systems that have drifted, malfunctioned, or evolved in unexpected ways?
- Do any characters interact directly with these systems?

## [[6adf0130-83d0-4b37-a8a2-62f745dc64c4|Ubiquitous Particle Dust]]

- How are the particles manufactured and replenished?
- Is the dust distributed evenly across the city, or concentrated in specific zones?
- Are citizens aware of the particle dust's existence?
- Can the dust be weaponized or subverted?
- Is the dust present beyond the city walls?

## [[5b2b1e73-7396-44c0-aedc-18ff88db970b|Weather Control]]

- Who are the human operators — a dedicated team, a government body, a corporation?
- How far in advance can the AI reliably predict weather?
- Are there limits to what weather states can be achieved or maintained?
- What happens if the system fails — does natural weather return immediately?
- Could the system be used offensively (e.g. storms directed outward)?

### Scope discrepancy to resolve
The original AI Infrastructure overview said weather control operated "within and around the city". The dedicated Weather Control entry said control applied above Atlantis only, as far as was known, and that effects beyond its boundaries were unknown. The lore entries retain the common established claim—control over Atlantis—and leave operation or effects outside it unresolved. This is a question for the author, not an expansion of the system's range.

## [[d65f2742-9501-4833-9ef1-ccefd89cf437|Energy Supply]] and [[7b7ed580-87f2-435d-b262-b39bd1ac14b4|Matter Creation]]

- Is energy-to-mass conversion via fusion reactors actually physically plausible at city scale? Needs research.
- What is the minimum fuel input required to power a city of Atlantis's scale, and how is that fuel sourced or replenished?

Heavy water or a deuterium/tritium mix were suggested as possible reactor fuel; no fuel composition or replenishment system is established. The original wording proposed sustaining the city indefinitely from small inputs, but the engineering and supply requirements need definition.

Matter creation is the intended fictional explanation for material abundance. Its physical plausibility and mechanism have not been demonstrated; this restructuring adds no scientific explanation.
