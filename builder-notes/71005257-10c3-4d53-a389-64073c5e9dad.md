Questions about social life and culture. Questions and proposed tensions do not establish world facts.

## [[056ae6dd-1c03-4b3a-9e2a-f59082dab862|Character & Attitude]]

- Does anyone in the city have genuine self-awareness about this collective overconfidence?
- Are there subcultures or individuals who push back against the ease — who seek out real challenge or discomfort?
- How does this attitude crack under genuine, unexpected pressure?

## [[98da6ee9-c7fc-412e-8a86-31ff8265dc60|Arts & Style]]

- Are there recognized tastemakers, artists, or style icons who hold significant social influence?
- Is there a tension between "old" established taste (perhaps tied to the founding elite) and newer, more subversive aesthetics?
- What art forms are most prominent — visual art, music, fashion, architecture, performance?
- Is bad taste merely looked down upon, or does it carry real social consequences?

## [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]]

- Is wealth distributed evenly, or is there a visible gap between the richest and the rest?
- What does "poor" even look like in Atlantis — is there a lower class, and if so, how do they live?
- What is the source of this wealth? (Industry, resources, trade — even if there's nobody to trade with?)

The earlier question about the source of abundance has a partial answer in [[d65f2742-9501-4833-9ef1-ccefd89cf437|Energy Supply]] and [[7b7ed580-87f2-435d-b262-b39bd1ac14b4|Matter Creation]]. Distribution, limits, and supply details remain open; do not treat trade or resource extraction as established explanations.

## [[4a15d972-ca62-4a44-aaf5-d1b61c2b25ef|Work & Leisure]]

- How many hours a day does the average person actually work?
