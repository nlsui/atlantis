Questions and provisional organizational details. These are author tasks rather than descriptions of established institutions.

## [[0f59ea4e-12df-4128-a574-00c64ef20f33|Government]]

- Are there multiple corporations sharing power, or is one dominant?
- Is there any democratic facade — elections, councils — or is corporate rule open and uncontested?
- Do citizens understand and accept this system, or is there tension/resistance?
- What do these corporations actually do — are they industry-specific or more like ruling families?
- Is there any form of public governance at all, even symbolic?

The existing account establishes a small number of powerful corporations, but the balance among them and whether one dominates remain unresolved.

## [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|The Defense Force]]

- What is the defense force called — is there an official name?
- What is the academy called?
- How are recruits selected — volunteers, drafted, identified by ability?
- What does futuristic combat look like here — weapons, augmentations, techniques?
- What is the social status of defenders in Atlantis? Celebrated heroes? Quietly necessary?
- How many active groups are there?
- Is there a hierarchy within and between groups?
- Do defenders ever go beyond monster-fighting — policing, politics?

### Provisional structure and story use
- Small defender groups, likely fewer than ten members, were proposed as a useful storytelling cast size; the precise organization is not established.
- Multiple groups are likely, but their number and organization remain undefined.
- Defense Academy is a descriptive working title, not an established official name.

## [[4ff3b1f5-8246-48e2-95b3-d95432d1b822|Education]]

- At what age does the path assessment begin?
- Is the AI that handles assessments one of the city's main infrastructure systems, or a separate one?
- Do corporations actively compete to attract promising young people?
- What happens to people who never find a clear path — or who reject the system entirely?
- Is there any form of general education before the assessment, or is even early childhood learning guided?
