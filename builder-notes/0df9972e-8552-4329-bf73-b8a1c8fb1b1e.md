## Established foundation

The public historical account covers genetic modification, resulting unrest, war between modified and unmodified humans, victory for the unmodified side, and the belief that only unmodified humans remain. The [[7bf708eb-a5e9-404a-9ba5-a475fcae28dc|End of the Gene Wars]] marks year zero. The later ban covers all human genetic modification and research toward it, including medical treatments.

[[54cc7097-07e0-4723-9c1a-d1ec7e8030ee|Time and Calendar Conventions]] places the present at approximately year 1449. The wars are in the future relative to real-world 2026, but ended 1,449 years before Atlantis's present; no exact real-world date is established.

## Framing to preserve

The conflict arises from the unrest caused by altering human traits, including strength and lifespan. It is not being defined as a simple, fixed two-class society or as a superhero premise. Do not invent named factions, campaigns, a founding history for Atlantis, or further hidden explanations. The human origins of the monsters and the worldwide devastation are now established hidden facts, as recorded below.

## Established hidden history

The Gene Wars were worldwide and wartime weapons devastated most of Earth. This devastation is part of the background to Atlantis's belief that it is the only city. Atlantis is the only established city for now; whether other cities survive elsewhere remains undecided.

The monsters outside Atlantis are mutated humans originating from genetically modified survivors of the Gene Wars. Their existence directly results from the war. This is all-knowing-wiki knowledge, not inhabitants' knowledge.

## Tentative weapons idea

Radiation from weapons used during the war may have contributed to further mutation or faster subsequent change in already genetically modified humans. This is a working idea only. The actual weapons and the mechanism of mutation have not been decided; do not present radiation or accelerated evolution as established lore.

## Low-priority details

Whether a monster is an original survivor of the Gene Wars or belongs to a later generation can be decided case by case for a particular character or monster type. Both possibilities may be used; no universal choice is required, and this is not a priority for current worldbuilding.
