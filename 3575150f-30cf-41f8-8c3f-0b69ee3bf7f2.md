The monsters in the wilderness are mutated humans whose origins lie in genetically modified humans who survived the [[2b6cc881-dbd8-476e-b153-e4ae556f10bc|Gene Wars]]. Their existence is a direct result of the war.

Their human origins and the survival of genetically modified humans are hidden from inhabitants of Atlantis. The publicly accepted account that only unmodified humans remain does not reveal this truth.
