The [[2b6cc881-dbd8-476e-b153-e4ae556f10bc|Gene Wars]] were a worldwide conflict, not a war confined to Atlantis. Weapons used during the conflict devastated most of Earth.

This devastation is behind the circumstances in which inhabitants of [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]] believe their city is the only city in the world. The worldwide scale of the destruction and this historical context are not common knowledge among inhabitants.
