Architects are the designers entrusted with the aesthetic and atmosphere of [[d28e126f-5ce7-412d-ade3-26c5b955f079|Quarters]] in [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]]. Their authority covers buildings, streets, materials, lighting, and local atmospheric effects.

## Creative direction
An Architect is not a draftsman in the traditional sense — nobody is sketching blueprints or laying bricks by hand. The role is closer to a creative director: an Architect describes a vision, reacts to what comes back, and refines it through conversation and feedback with an AI system that handles the actual technical realization — structure, materials, logistics, dust behavior.

This is the same intention-and-execution pattern used elsewhere in the city (see [[5b2b1e73-7396-44c0-aedc-18ff88db970b|Weather Control]] and [[bf823195-c261-4d27-9649-b2c4970787c8|AI Infrastructure]]): the human supplies the taste and the direction, the AI supplies the complexity.

## Materials and atmosphere
Because materials are abundant (see [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]]) and [[7b7ed580-87f2-435d-b262-b39bd1ac14b4|Matter Creation]] supplies physical resources from the city's fusion energy, Architects are rarely constrained by cost or scarcity. If a vision calls for marble, or timber, or something stranger, it can generally be built. The limiting factor on a quarter's design is imagination and taste — not budget.

Architects don't just build structures — they direct atmosphere. The city's [[6adf0130-83d0-4b37-a8a2-62f745dc64c4|Ubiquitous Particle Dust]] can be tuned locally within a quarter to reinforce whatever mood or era the Architect is evoking: rolling fog, a particular quality of light, a chill in the air, even faint ambient sound.

## Lead Architects and sub-architects
A quarter is rarely one person's work alone. The lead Architect owns and drives the overall vision, but sub-architects are entrusted with sub-areas — a street, a block, a district-within-the-district — where they work the same conversational, AI-directed way, in service of the lead's overall vision rather than their own. Rising from sub-architect to lead Architect of one's own quarter is itself a step on the same prestige ladder.

## Recognition
Architects earn their roles through acclaim for their creative work and taste, rather than by buying the position. The [[b0729f42-efb8-4df0-bcac-3da7c46d08e4|Architect Guild]] formally assigns quarters and sub-areas, recognizing designers' prestige. The more acclaimed the designer, the more or larger territory they can be entrusted to shape.
