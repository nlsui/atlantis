Architects are the designers entrusted with the aesthetic and atmosphere of [[d28e126f-5ce7-412d-ade3-26c5b955f079|Quarters]] in [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]]. Their authority covers buildings, streets, materials, lighting, and local atmospheric effects.

## Design freedom

Because materials are abundant, Architects are rarely constrained by cost or scarcity. Marble, timber, or stranger materials can generally be used. Imagination and taste, rather than budget, limit a quarter's design.

Architects direct atmosphere as well as structures: fog, lighting, ambient chill, and sound can reinforce the mood or era of a quarter.

## Lead Architects and sub-architects

A quarter is rarely one person's work alone. The lead Architect owns and drives the overall vision, but sub-architects are entrusted with sub-areas — a street, a block, a district-within-the-district — where they work the same conversational way, in service of the lead's overall vision rather than their own. Rising from sub-architect to lead Architect of one's own quarter is itself a step on the same prestige ladder.

## Recognition

Architects earn their roles through acclaim for their creative work and taste, rather than by buying the position. The [[b0729f42-efb8-4df0-bcac-3da7c46d08e4|Architect Guild]] formally assigns quarters and sub-areas, recognizing designers' prestige. The more acclaimed the designer, the more or larger territory they can be entrusted to shape.
