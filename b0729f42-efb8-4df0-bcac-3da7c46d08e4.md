The Architect Guild is the self-governing, peer-run body that assigns quarters and sub-areas to [[0d15b29c-79fd-495c-9454-0cc963fe08a1|Architects]]. It is not a corporate or state office.

## Assignment and acclaim
The Guild recognizes creative acclaim and formally entrusts designers with territory. Recognition and taste, rather than wealth, determine advancement; greater acclaim can bring responsibility for more or larger areas.

## Unclaimed territory
A quarter becomes effectively unclaimed when it empties faster than its Architect can reinvent it and the Architect no longer has the drive to continue. An aspiring Architect can stake a claim, typically with the Guild ratifying the handover. New territory that has never been shaped starts out unclaimed in the same way.
