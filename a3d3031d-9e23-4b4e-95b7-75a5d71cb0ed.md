The Defense Academy is the dedicated academy within [[7d3e16fc-b364-4b58-b06d-b641891452cb|City of Atlantis]] that trains the city's [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]]. It is the pipeline through which all defenders pass.

Training is rigorous and produces highly skilled, technologically augmented fighters whose futuristic combat styles differ from conventional military tradition.
