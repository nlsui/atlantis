The people of Atlantis are, as a rule, confident. Deeply, naturally, almost constitutionally confident.

And why wouldn't they be? They live in [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]]. The [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]] handles the [[0a33431b-a93e-439c-9904-997bbe09e3fe|Monsters]] outside the walls. The city runs smoothly. Style and taste are the primary arenas of life — and people in Atlantis genuinely believe they have both. There is very little that feels threatening or uncertain about existence here.

## The Festival

At [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]], [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]] offers two days of life without the usual technological assistance each year. It is experienced as a fun, novel break, and attendees return gratefully to the ease outside the dome.
