The people of Atlantis are, as a rule, confident. Deeply, naturally, almost constitutionally confident.

And why wouldn't they be? They live in [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]]. The [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]] handles the [[0a33431b-a93e-439c-9904-997bbe09e3fe|Monsters]] outside the walls. The city runs smoothly. Style and taste are the primary arenas of life — and people in Atlantis genuinely believe they have both. There is very little that feels threatening or uncertain about existence here.

## The Subtle Edge of Overconfidence

What the inhabitants don't fully reckon with is how much of their ease is *given* to them. [[bf823195-c261-4d27-9649-b2c4970787c8|AI Infrastructure]] handles the complexity of the city behind the scenes. Difficult problems are quietly solved before most people ever notice them. The result is a population that has rarely been tested by genuine adversity — and doesn't know it.

This doesn't manifest as arrogance in any obvious or villainous way. It's subtler than that. It's the quiet assumption that things will work out. The lack of existential worry. The unexamined certainty that they are, in fact, as good as they feel. Life is good, they are stylish, and the world is largely as it should be.

It is a blind spot worn lightly, like a very expensive coat.

## The Festival

At [[aa6eda8e-963e-4dcc-9af9-5145cd8c9527|The Dome]], [[4719bd14-62a2-438e-aad9-2896fd76a363|The Festival]] offers two days of life without AI assistance each year. It is experienced as a fun, novel break rather than a wake-up call about dependence. Attendees return gratefully to the ease outside the dome; knowing precisely when the experience ends helps reinforce the belief that they can handle life without AI.
