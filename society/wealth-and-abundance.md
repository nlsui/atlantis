# Wealth & Abundance

Atlantis is an extraordinarily wealthy city. Resources are plentiful, infrastructure is excellent, and the general standard of living is high. Scarcity, as most people might imagine it, is not a visible feature of daily life in Atlantis.

This abundance shapes the culture profoundly — the expectations people have, the way they spend their time, what they consider normal or desirable. It is simply the water they swim in.

Atlantis is, in the truest sense, a utopia. This is not a cyberpunk dystopia where corporations squeeze the population — corporate power exists, but the city is genuinely good to live in. The abundance is real, and people feel it.

## Open Questions
- Is wealth distributed evenly, or is there a visible gap between the richest and the rest?
- What does "poor" even look like in Atlantis — is there a lower class, and if so, how do they live?
- What is the source of this wealth? (Industry, resources, trade — even if there's nobody to trade with?)
