# Ubiquitous Particle Dust

Atlantis is permeated by an invisible layer of nanoscale particle modules — so small and so dispersed that they are undetectable to the naked eye. This is the physical substrate through which much of the city's AI infrastructure operates.

## What the Particles Do
Each particle is a specialized nano-module. Different particle types serve different roles:
- **Sensing** — reading environmental data: temperature, humidity, pressure, location, movement, and more
- **Transmission** — relaying signals and data across the particle network
- **Actuation** — physically affecting the environment (e.g. seeding clouds, altering local air composition)

Together, the dust forms a city-wide sensory and actuation mesh — a nervous system for Atlantis that no individual could perceive or operate manually.

## The Role of AI
The particle network generates data and complexity at a scale no human can directly process or control. This is where AI becomes essential. The AI systems of Atlantis serve as the intelligence layer on top of the dust — interpreting incoming data, modeling outcomes, and orchestrating particle behavior to achieve desired effects.

Human operators interact with the AI, not the dust directly. They set intentions; the AI figures out how to make the particles execute them.

## Local Use by Architects
Beyond city-wide systems like weather control, the dust can also be tuned locally within a single quarter — fog, light quality, ambient chill, and more — to reinforce the aesthetic vision of that quarter's Architect. See [Quarters & Architects](../../geography/city/quarters.md).

## The One Exception
[The Dome](../../geography/city/the-dome.md), a large park elsewhere in the city, is deliberately kept free of particle dust entirely — the only place in Atlantis with no dust presence at all. It's preserved this way on purpose, not an oversight.
