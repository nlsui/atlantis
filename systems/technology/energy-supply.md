# Energy Supply

Atlantis runs on fusion power. Its reactors represent a generational leap beyond anything in our contemporary world — producing effectively limitless, clean energy from minimal fuel input. The city's abundance, its AI infrastructure, and its technological standard of living all rest on this foundation.

The reactors require only small quantities of fuel (likely heavy water or a deuterium/tritium mix) to sustain output for the entire city indefinitely.

## Matter Creation
The fusion reactors are also the source of Atlantis's material abundance. At sufficient scale, the energy output is used for matter creation — converting energy into physical resources. This is the intended explanation for how the city sustains its wealth and abundance without conventional resource extraction.
