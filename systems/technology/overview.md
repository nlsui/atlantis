# Technology

Atlantis is a futuristic city. Its technology is advanced well beyond what we'd consider contemporary — evident in its AI infrastructure, its resource abundance, and the combat capabilities of its defense force.

Technology is so embedded in daily life that it is largely invisible. It is the foundation the utopia rests on.

## Open Questions
- What is the general aesthetic of the technology — sleek and minimal, organic, industrial?
- Are there visible signs of advanced tech in everyday life (transport, buildings, communication)?
- Who develops and maintains the technology — corporations, the AI systems themselves?
- Is there any technology that is restricted or inaccessible to ordinary citizens?
