# Weather Control

Atlantis maintains precise, deliberate control over its own weather. The system operates above the city and — as far as is known — does not extend beyond its boundaries.

## How It Works
The weather control system is a collaboration between AI, human operators, and the ubiquitous particle dust.

1. **The particle dust** continuously reads atmospheric data across the city — temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, and more
2. **The AI** ingests this data, models weather patterns, and predicts upcoming conditions (e.g. "rain in two hours if nothing changes")
3. **Human operators** review the AI's predictions and issue high-level directives ("make it sunny")
4. **The AI** then orchestrates the particle dust to enact the necessary atmospheric changes to achieve the desired outcome

The human operator sets the intention. The AI handles the complexity of execution.

## Scope
Weather control is applied above Atlantis only — at least as far as anyone knows. Whether it has any effect on surrounding regions, or could be extended, is unknown.

## Open Questions
- Who are the human operators — a dedicated team, a government body, a corporation?
- How far in advance can the AI reliably predict weather?
- Are there limits to what weather states can be achieved or maintained?
- What happens if the system fails — does natural weather return immediately?
- Could the system be used offensively (e.g. storms directed outward)?
