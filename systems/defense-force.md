# The Defense Force

Atlantis maintains an organized corps of trained defenders whose purpose is to protect the city from the monsters that inhabit the outside world. They are the thin line between the utopia inside the walls and the danger beyond them.

## The Academy
Defenders are trained at a dedicated academy within the city. Training is rigorous and produces fighters with futuristic combat styles — highly skilled, technologically augmented, and unlike anything from conventional military tradition.

## Combat as Art

The defenders are, technologically and physically, vastly superior to the monsters they fight. Victory is not in doubt. What is in doubt — and what matters deeply — is *how* you win.

Fighting with style, elegance, and flair is how defenders earn honor and reputation. Techniques that look extraordinary, moves that are unnecessarily risky but breathtaking — these are celebrated. A defender who dispatches monsters efficiently but plainly is respected. A defender who makes it beautiful is legendary.

This is entirely consistent with the broader culture of Atlantis: excellence and taste over mere function.

## The Vibe

Going out to fight monsters is not a terrifying ordeal. It is closer to a high-stakes football match on the weekend — exhausting, challenging, and genuinely demanding, but also exciting and fun. Defenders go out with confidence, even eagerness. There is danger, but it is chosen and relished, not feared. A good outing is something to be proud of and talked about.

This shapes the character of defenders profoundly. They are confident — often supremely so. They are performers as much as soldiers. The battlefield is, in a sense, their stage.

## Structure
- Defenders operate in small groups (likely under 10 per unit), which makes them well-suited as a cast for storytelling
- Multiple groups likely exist — the exact number and organization is to be defined
- The academy is the pipeline through which all defenders pass
