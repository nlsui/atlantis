# Education

Education in Atlantis is personal, AI-guided, and deeply tied to the corporations that form the backbone of the city.

## The Path Assessment

At a certain age, every child undergoes an evaluation process. This is not a single test — it is an ongoing conversation. An AI system monitors the child over time: their interests, instincts, skills, and preferences. It observes as much as it asks. The result is a suggested path — a recommended direction for education and eventual career.

The suggestion carries weight and is taken seriously, but it is not a sentence. People can change direction at any point. If interests shift, if a new passion emerges, the path can be revised. The system adapts.

## Corporation-Tied Education

There is no general school in the traditional sense. Once a path is suggested, education is tailored specifically toward it — and toward the corporation or field it leads to. Learning is practical, contextual, and purposeful from early on. By the time someone enters a corporation, they have been shaped for it.

This means corporations are not just employers — they are present in a person's life from a young age, through the educational pipeline they sponsor or run.

## Work & Leisure

Because resources are so abundant, work occupies only a small part of the day. People are productive, but briefly. The majority of daily life is free time — for art, leisure, socializing, personal pursuits. A career is an identity and a source of pride, but it is not the dominant feature of how time is spent.

- Is there any form of general education before the assessment, or is even early childhood learning guided?
