# AI Infrastructure

Atlantis runs on AI — but quietly. There is no single omniscient system overseeing everything. Instead, the city is maintained by a network of specialized AI systems, each responsible for a specific domain. Together they make the city function with a seamlessness that most inhabitants simply take for granted.

The AI layer is not secret, exactly, but it is not obvious either. It is so deeply woven into the fabric of everyday life that it rarely draws attention. People don't think of it as "AI running the city" — they just experience smooth streets, pleasant weather, and a safe environment.
