# Government

Atlantis is not governed democratically. Power lies with corporations — a small number of powerful entities that effectively control the city's infrastructure, resources, and decision-making. The result is something closer to a corporate oligarchy than a civic government.

## Open Questions
- Are there multiple corporations sharing power, or is one dominant?
- Is there any democratic facade — elections, councils — or is corporate rule open and uncontested?
- Do citizens understand and accept this system, or is there tension/resistance?
- What do these corporations actually do — are they industry-specific or more like ruling families?
- Is there any form of public governance at all, even symbolic?
