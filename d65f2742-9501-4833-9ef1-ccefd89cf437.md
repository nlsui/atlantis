Atlantis runs on fusion power. Its reactors represent a generational leap beyond contemporary technology, producing effectively limitless clean energy from minimal fuel input.

The city's abundance, [[bf823195-c261-4d27-9649-b2c4970787c8|AI Infrastructure]], and technological standard of living rest on this foundation. The energy output also powers [[7b7ed580-87f2-435d-b262-b39bd1ac14b4|Matter Creation]].
