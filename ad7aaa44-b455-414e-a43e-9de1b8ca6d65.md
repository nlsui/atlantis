Atlantis is a futuristic city. Its technology is advanced well beyond what we would consider contemporary, evident in its [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]] and the combat capabilities of [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|the defense force]].

Technology is so embedded in daily life that it is largely invisible. It is the foundation the utopia rests on.
