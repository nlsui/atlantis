Atlantis is a futuristic city. Its technology is advanced well beyond what we'd consider contemporary — evident in its [[bf823195-c261-4d27-9649-b2c4970787c8|AI Infrastructure]], its [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]], and the combat capabilities of its [[10a29d1c-2a74-41fe-a8ae-678be2ee5283|defense force]].

Technology is so embedded in daily life that it is largely invisible. It is the foundation the utopia rests on.
