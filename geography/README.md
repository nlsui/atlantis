# Geography

This section covers the physical world of Atlantis — from the layout of the city itself to the lands and waters that lie beyond its boundaries.

## Structure

### `city/`
Everything about Atlantis as a city: its overall layout, districts, quarters, landmarks, infrastructure, and the character of its different areas.

### `beyond-the-city/`
The known world outside the city walls — surrounding terrain, regions, settlements, natural features, and anything else that has been explored or documented.

### `ideas-and-open-questions/`
A holding space for half-formed ideas, loose concepts, and unresolved questions about geography. Things land here when they're interesting but not yet fully defined — revisit and promote to the appropriate folder once they've been fleshed out.
