# Open Questions — Beyond the City

## Mountains
- How many mountains are there?
- Do they have names?
- Where are they positioned relative to the city — backdrop to one side, surrounding on multiple sides?
- Are they accessible / do people venture into them?

## Coastline
- How close is the sea to the city walls?
- Is there a harbor, beach, cliffs?
- Does the coastline run along one side of the city or wrap around it?

## Grasslands & Forest
- What is the rough ratio of open grass to forest?
- Are they separate zones (e.g. forest to the north, plains to the south) or intermixed?
- Are there any notable landmarks within the nature — rivers, lakes, clearings?

## General
- Is the nature outside the city protected or wild? Are there people living out there?
- How far does the "known" world extend before it becomes uncharted territory?
