# The Dome (placeholder name)

Somewhere in the city — its exact location isn't important to the lore — stands a large park enclosed by a dome. It is the only place in Atlantis with no particle dust at all: no sensing, no actuation, no AI-mediated anything. This is deliberate and preserved on purpose, not an oversight or an anomaly.

## The Park
- A genuine green park — trees, open grass, space to breathe — not an ornamental garden
- Large enough to comfortably host enormous crowds
- Enclosed by the dome, which is what excludes the particle dust from the space

## Why It's Preserved
The dust-free state is maintained deliberately, not accidental. The reasoning and history behind its preservation are still to be defined (see Open Questions) — likely tied to the city's founding.

## The Festival (placeholder name)

Once a year, for two days, the park hosts "the Festival" — a highly anticipated, deeply traditional gathering. For those two days, people inside the dome experience life with zero AI or particle-dust assistance: nothing is sensed, predicted, or quietly handled in the background. Everything is unmediated.

This isn't experienced as hardship so much as a rare, prized occasion — the direct inverse of daily life's frictionless ease, valued precisely because it's the only place and time it happens. It's a striking reminder of just how much the AI infrastructure normally does, since two days without it is remarkable enough to be the city's biggest yearly tradition. See [Character & Attitude](../../society/character-and-attitude.md) for how this sits alongside the city's broader (and largely unexamined) relationship with ease.

## Open Questions
- What are the park and the festival actually called?
- Who founded or preserves the dome, and is that history documented anywhere?
- What do people actually do during the festival — structured rituals and games, or something more free-form?
- Is attendance universal, by invitation, by lottery, or simply open to whoever shows up?
- Does anything need to be arranged practically to run the city (or just that zone) without AI assistance for two days?
- Does the disconnection extend to personal devices people bring in, or only to the ambient city infrastructure?
- Is the festival universally embraced, or is there any tension/resistance to it?
