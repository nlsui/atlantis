# Monsters

Beyond the city walls, the nature is not just beautiful — it is dangerous. Monsters inhabit the outside world, making the wilderness a genuine threat to the city and anyone who ventures into it.

The existence of monsters is the primary reason Atlantis maintains an organized defense force. They are the conflict at the edge of the utopia.

## Open Questions
- What do the monsters look like — are they animals, creatures, something stranger?
- Are they numerous, rare, or somewhere in between?
- Do they actively approach the city, or must defenders go out to meet them?
- Do they have intelligence, or are they purely instinct-driven?
- Is their origin known — natural, artificial, ancient?
- Have they always been there, or is their presence something that changed over time?
