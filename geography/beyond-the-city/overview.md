# Beyond the City — Overview

Once past the walls of Atlantis, the city gives way to open, largely unspoiled nature. The surrounding landscape is a mix of rolling grasslands and forest, with mountains rising nearby and the sea within reach.

## Key Features

### The Sea
Atlantis is situated near the coast. The sea is a defining feature of the city's identity and likely plays a role in its culture, trade, and history.

### The Mountains
One or more mountains rise in the vicinity of the city, providing dramatic elevation and geographical contrast. Their exact number, names, and distance from the city are still to be defined.

### Grasslands & Forest
The land around Atlantis is a blend of open grass and woodland. The intention is that stepping beyond the city walls feels like stepping into beautiful, living nature — a clear contrast to the urban world inside.
