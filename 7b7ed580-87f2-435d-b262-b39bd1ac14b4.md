Energy from the city's [[d65f2742-9501-4833-9ef1-ccefd89cf437|Energy Supply]] is used to create matter, converting energy into physical resources. This supplies material abundance without conventional resource extraction.

This capability supports [[ab11744a-9b3d-4c17-82d6-04003387a140|Wealth & Abundance]] and gives [[0d15b29c-79fd-495c-9454-0cc963fe08a1|Architects]] broad freedom to realize their designs with marble, timber, or other materials without ordinary constraints of cost and scarcity.
