The Dome is deliberately kept free of [[6adf0130-83d0-4b37-a8a2-62f745dc64c4|Ubiquitous Particle Dust]]. The dome enclosure excludes the particles, leaving the park without sensing, actuation, or AI-mediated assistance. It is the only place in Atlantis without particle dust.
