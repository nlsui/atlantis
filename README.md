# Atlantis

Atlantis is a world-building project — a collaborative effort to construct a vast, detailed fictional world from the ground up.

## What This Is

This knowledge base is the living foundation of the world of Atlantis. It captures everything that makes the world real: its landscapes, cultures, peoples, notable characters, governments, histories, and more. As the world grows, so does this repository.

## Goals

The immediate goal is to build a rich, well-organized knowledge base. Over time, this world may serve as the foundation for stories, animations, or other creative work — but for now, the focus is on defining and documenting the world itself.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Geography** — The city's layout and the lands beyond its walls, including coastline, mountains, and nature
- **Society** — The soft fabric of life: culture, traditions, religion, arts, daily life, and social norms
- **Systems** — The hard structures: government, economy, law, military, and institutions
- **Characters** — Notable individuals, their backgrounds, roles, and stories
- **Stories** — Narratives set in the world of Atlantis, drawing on the knowledge base
- **History** — The events, eras, and turning points that shaped the world
- **Open Questions** — Cross-cutting ideas and unresolved questions that don't yet have a home

## How It's Built

This knowledge base grows through conversation and collaboration. New lore is added as it's created, refined, and organized into a consistent and navigable whole.
