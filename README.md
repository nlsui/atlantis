# Atlantis

Atlantis is a collaborative worldbuilding project. This repository holds the world's places, people, culture, institutions, technology, and history, alongside ideas for its future development.

## Explore the world

- [All-knowing wiki](https://nlsui.github.io/atlantis/wiki/) — the established lore, including underlying mechanisms and facts that ordinary inhabitants may not know.
- [Atlantis inhabitants wiki](https://nlsui.github.io/atlantis/atlantis-inhabitants/) — commonly available knowledge within Atlantis.

The inhabitants' wiki reflects general knowledge, rather than everything a particular individual might know. Familiar uses of AI, such as education and artistic work, belong here; the particle network and the underlying city-wide infrastructure currently appear only in the all-knowing wiki.

Both wikis are publicly accessible. Their different perspectives represent knowledge within the fictional world.

## In this repository

- **Lore entries** describe established subjects and their relationships. Additional details can appear as sections of the same wiki page without duplicating the main description.
- **[Builder notes](builder-notes/)** collect open questions, to-dos, inspirations, and provisional decisions, including calendar conventions. They are excluded from both wikis.
