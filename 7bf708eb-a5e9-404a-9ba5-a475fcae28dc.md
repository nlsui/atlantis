The unmodified humans won the Gene Wars, also called the Genetic Wars. According to publicly accepted history, only unmodified humans remain in the present.

The end of the wars marks year zero of the calendar used in Atlantis. The current reference present is approximately year 1449, placing the end of the wars 1,449 years earlier. This history is common knowledge among Atlantis inhabitants.
